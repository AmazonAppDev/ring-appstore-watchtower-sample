## What this app does

[App name] shows the Ring cameras you choose to share on one screen, as live video. You connect it
from the Ring Appstore in the Ring app. Ring asks which devices to share and asks for your consent
before [App name] can access anything.

## What we collect and how

| Data | How we get it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your Ring account ID | From Ring, when you install the app (`GET /v1/users/me`) | To link your Ring account to your [App name] account and to match Ring notifications to you |
| Your email address | From Ring, when you install the app. You also type it when you sign back in | To create your account, show which account is linked, and send sign-in codes |
| Ring access and refresh tokens | From Ring, when you install the app | To read your shared devices and stream their live video for you |
| A 6-digit sign-in code | Generated by us when you ask to sign in | To verify it's you. Each code is single use |
| Webhook delivery IDs | From Ring notifications | To avoid processing the same notification twice |
| Service logs | Generated when you use the app | To operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. Logs include internal user IDs, Ring account IDs, and Ring camera (device) IDs, for example when a stream fails or a Ring notification arrives. They never include tokens or video |

**What we don't collect or store:**
- **Video.** Live video travels directly between your browser and Ring. It is never recorded or stored by us.
- **Device lists, device status and subscription status.** These are read live from Ring each time you open the dashboard, and are not stored.
- **Passwords.** [App name] doesn't use passwords.
- **Analytics, advertising or tracking data.**

## How we use your data

Only to provide the features you consented to in the Ring app: showing your shared cameras and
their status, streaming live video, showing your plan status, and signing you in. We don't use
your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to those features. We don't combine
it with data from other Ring accounts or other sources.

## Who we share it with

- **Ring (Amazon).** We send requests to Ring's API on your behalf, using your tokens.
- **Service providers that host the app for us**, acting only on our instructions: Amazon Web
  Services (hosting, database, logging, and Amazon SES for sign-in emails). [List any others you add.]
- **Google's public STUN server** (`stun.l.google.com`). When live video starts, your browser
  contacts this server to learn its public network address, so it can connect directly to Ring.
  Google receives your browser's **IP address and port**, nothing else: no video, account, email or
  camera information. Ring's live-streaming guide recommends this server. *Publishers: to avoid
  this third party, run your own STUN/TURN server (for example on AWS) and set it in
  `frontend/src/webrtc.js`, then update this section.*

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires us to.

| Sign-in codes | Used, or 10 minutes, whichever comes first |
| Webhook delivery IDs | 24 hours |
| Service logs | **[Retention period, for example 30 days]**. *Publishers: the sample doesn't set a CloudWatch log retention, and the default is "never expire". Set one.* |

## Your choices: deletion and withdrawing consent

- **Delete your data in the app.** Open the ⋮ menu and choose **Delete my account & data**. This
  immediately deletes your account, your Ring tokens and any pending sign-in code. Your Ring
  account, devices and recordings aren't affected.
- **Withdraw consent in Ring.** Remove [App name] in **Ring My Apps** in the Ring app. Ring then
  revokes our access, and we automatically delete every account, Ring token, pending link and
  sign-in code we hold for your Ring account.
- In both cases, service logs and webhook delivery IDs aren't deleted on request. They expire on
  the schedule above.
- **Change which cameras are shared** at any time in the Ring app.

  within **[time frame]**. [Add any rights that apply in your jurisdiction, such as GDPR or CCPA.]
